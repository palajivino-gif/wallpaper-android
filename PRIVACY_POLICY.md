# Danzy Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

Danzy is a wallpaper application. This policy explains how the app handles data.

## Data stored on the device

Danzy stores favorite-wallpaper selections locally on the user's device. The app can save a selected wallpaper to the device gallery and can stage a temporary image in the app cache when the user chooses to share it. Danzy does not operate its own server or require a user account.

## Advertising

Danzy uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google and its partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, diagnostic information, advertising interactions, and approximate location derived from the IP address to provide, measure, and protect advertising. Google's handling of this information is governed by Google's policies.

The development version uses Google's sample ad units. Production advertising must not be enabled until the required consent flow, Play Console disclosures, and AdMob settings have been completed.

## Permissions

Danzy uses internet access for advertising. It uses wallpaper access when the user chooses to apply an image. On Android 9 and earlier, storage permission may be requested only when the user chooses to save an image. Newer Android versions use the system media store without that legacy permission.

## Children's privacy

Danzy is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The production app's target-audience and advertising settings must be configured before release.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's features, advertising configuration, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
